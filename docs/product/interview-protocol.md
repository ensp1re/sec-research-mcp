# Interview protocol (F002)

Live interviews are required. Do not invent quotes or attendees.

## Target

At least six people across three groups from [PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md): individual researchers, analysts/journalists, and developers.

## Tasks to run with each person

1. Resolve a company and explain a five-year change.
2. Compare two companies over several fiscal periods, including a missing metric.
3. Ask what evidence they need before trusting a chart.

## Record

For each session: date, role group, task outcome, what failed, what they would pay for, and whether they would use a local MCP server.

F002 stays blocked until those notes exist. Contract work (F004+) may proceed using the personas already written in the product spec; interview findings can revise UX later.
